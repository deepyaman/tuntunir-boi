Grihôsther ghôrer pichhone begun gachh achhe. Shei begun gachher pata ṭhõṭ diye shelai kôre Ṭunṭuni pakhiṭi tar basha bẽdhechhe.

Bashar bhitore tinṭi chhoṭṭo-chhoṭṭo chhana hoyechhe. Khub chhoṭṭo chhana, tara uṛte pare na, chokho melte pare na. Khali hã kôre, ar chĩ-chĩ kôre.

Grihôsther biṛalṭa bhari dushṭo. She khali bhabe ‘Ṭunṭunir chhana khabo.’ Êkdin she begun gachher tôlay eshe bolle, ‘Ki korchhish la Ṭunṭuni?’

Ṭunṭuni tar matha hẽṭ kôre begun gachher ḍale ṭhekiye bolle, ‘Prônam hoi, Môharani!’

Tate biṛalni bhari khushi hoye chôle gêlo.

Emni she roj ashe, roj Ṭunṭuni take prônam kôre ar môharani bôle, ar she khushi hoye chôle jay.

Êkhon Ṭunṭunir chhanaguli bôṛo hoyechhe, tader shundor pakha hoyechhe. Tara ar chokh buje thake na. Ta dekhe Ṭunṭuni tader bolle, ‘Bachha, tora uṛte parbi?’

Chhanara bolle, ‘Hễ ma, parbo.’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe dêkh to dekhi, oi tal gachhṭar ḍale giye boshte parish kina.’

Chhanara tôkhoni uṛe giye tal gachher ḍale boshlo. Ta dekhe Ṭunṭuni heshe bolle, ‘Êkhon dushṭu biṛal ashuk dekhi!’

<Illustration src={page07} caption="‘Prônam hoi Môharani!’" alt="The cat sits beneath the eggplant bush, looking up at the tailorbird on a branch." />

Khanik badei biṛal eshe bolle, ‘Ki korchhish la Ṭunṭuni?’

Tôkhon Ṭunṭuni pa uṭhiye take lathi dekhiye bolle, ‘Dur hô, lokkhichhaṛi biṛalni!’ Bolei she phuṛuk kôre uṛe palalo.

<Illustration src={page08} caption="‘Dur hô, lokkhichhaṛi biṛalni!’" alt="The cat rears up snarling under the eggplant bush while the tailorbird sits safely on a branch above." />

Dushṭu biṛal dãt khĩchiye laphiye gachhe uṭhe, Ṭunṭunikeo dhorte parlo na, chhanao khete pelo na. Khali begun kãṭar khõcha kheye nakal hoye ghôre phirlo.
