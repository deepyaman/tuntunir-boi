Ṭunṭuni giyechhilo begun patay bôshe nachte. Nachte-nachte khelo begun kãṭar khõcha. Tai theke tar holo môsto bôṛo phoṛa.

O ma, ki hobe? Êto bôṛo phoṛa ki kôre sharbe?

Ṭunṭuni eke jiggesh kôre, take jiggesh kôre. Shôbai bolle, ‘Oṭa napit diye kaṭiye phêlo.’

Tai Ṭunṭuni napiter kachhe giye bolle, ‘Napitdada, Napitdada, amar phoṛaṭa keṭe dao na!’

Napit tar kôtha shune ghaṛ bẽkiye nak shĩṭkiye bolle, ‘Ish! Ami rajake kamai, ami tor phoṛa kaṭte gelum ar ki!’

<Illustration src={page10} caption="‘Ish! Ami rajake kamai, ami tor phoṛa kaṭte gelum ar ki!’" alt="The barber, razor in hand, turns up his nose at the tailorbird perched on a branch beside him." />

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Achchha, dekhte pabe êkhon, phoṛa kaṭte jao kina.’

Bôle she rajar kachhe giye nalish korle, ‘Rajamoshai, apnar napit kêno amar phoṛa keṭe dichchhe na? Oke shaja dite hobe.’

Shune rajamoshai ho-ho kôre hashlen, bichhanay gôṛagoṛi dilen, napitke kichhu bollen na. Tate, Ṭunṭunir bhari rag holo. She ĩdurer kachhe giye bolle, ‘Ĩdurbhai, Ĩdurbhai, baṛi achho?’

Ĩdur bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

<Illustration src={page11} caption="Raja ho-ho kôre hashlen." alt="The fat king in his turban rolls about laughing while the tailorbird watches from a branch." />

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi êk kaj kôro.’

Ĩdur bolle, ‘Ki kaj?’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Rajamoshai jôkhon ghumiye thakben, tôkhon giye tãr bhũṛiṭa keṭe phuṭo kôre dite hobe.’

Ta shune ĩdur jibh keṭe kane hat diye bolle, ‘Ore bapre! Ami ta parbo na.’ Tate Ṭunṭuni rag kôre biṛaler kachhe giye bolle, ‘Biṛalbhai, Biṛalbhai, baṛi achho?’

Biṛal bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

<Illustration src={page12} caption="‘Ore bapre! Ami ta parbo na.’" alt="The mouse sits up with a paw to its ear, aghast, as the tailorbird looks on." />

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi ĩdur maro.’

Biṛal bolle, ‘Êkhon ami ĩdur-ṭidur marte jete parbo na, amar bôḍḍo ghum peyechhe.’ Shune Ṭunṭuni rager bhôre laṭhir kachhe giye bolle, ‘Laṭhibhai, Laṭhibhai, baṛi achho?’

Laṭhi bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi biṛalke ṭhêngao.’

Laṭhi bolle, ‘Biṛal amar ki korechhe je ami take ṭhêngate jabo? Ami ta parbo na.’ Tôkhon Ṭunṭuni aguner kachhe giye bolle, ‘Agunbhai, Agunbhai, baṛi achho?’

<Illustration src={page13} caption="‘Ôto jôl khete parbo na, amar peṭ pheṭe jabe.’" alt="The tailorbird perches on the tip of an elephant's tusk; the elephant eyes it sidelong." />

Agun bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi tumi laṭhi poṛao.’

Agun bolle, ‘Aj ḍher jinish puṛiyechhi, aj ar kichhu poṛate parbo na.’ Tate Ṭunṭuni take khub kôre bôke, shagorer kachhe giye bolle, ‘Shagorbhai, Shagorbhai, baṛi achho?’

Shagor bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi tumi agun nibao.’

Shagor bolle, ‘Ami ta parbo na.’ Tôkhon Ṭunṭuni hatir kachhe giye bolle, ‘Hatibhai, Hatibhai, baṛi achho?’

<Illustration src={page14} caption="Môshay akash chheye gêlo." alt="A cloud of mosquitoes streams out of a triangle over the tailorbird on its branch." />

Hati bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi shagorer jôl shôb kheye phêlo.’

Hati bolle, ‘Ôto jôl khete parbo na, amar peṭ pheṭe jabe.’

Keu tar kôtha shunlo na dekhe Ṭunṭuni sheshe môshar kachhe gêlo. Môsha dur theke take dekhei bolle, ‘Ke bhai? Ṭunibhai? Esho bhai! Bôsho bhai! Khaṭ pete di, bhat beṛe di, khabe bhai?’

Ṭunṭuni bolle, ‘Tôbe bhat khai, jodi hatike kamṛao!’

Môsha bolle, ‘She abar êkṭa kôtha! Êkhuni jachchhi! Dekhbo hati bêṭar kôto shôkto chamṛa!’ Bôle, she shôkol desher shôkol môshake ḍeke bollo, ‘Tora ay to re bhai, dekhi hati bêṭar kôto shôkto chamṛa.’ Omni pin-pin-pin-pin kôre jôto rajjer môsha, bap bêṭa bhai bondhu mile hatike kamṛate chollo. Môshay akash chheye gêlo, shurjo ḍheke gêlo. Tader pakhar haoay jhôṛ boite laglo. Pin-pin-pin-pin bhôyanok shôbdo shune shôkoler pran kẽpe uṭhlo. Tôkhon—

Hati bôle, shagor shushi!
Shagor bôle, agun nebai!
Agun bôle, laṭhi poṛai!
Laṭhi bôle, biṛal ṭhêngai!
Biṛal bôle, ĩdur mari!
Ĩdur bôle, rajar bhũṛi kaṭi!
Raja bôle, napte bêṭar matha kaṭi!

Napit hat joṛ kôre kãpte-kãpte bolle, ‘Rokkhe kôro, Ṭunidada! Esho tomar phoṛa kaṭi.’

Tarpôr Ṭunṭunir phoṛa shere gêlo, ar she bhari khushi hoye abar giye nachte ar gaite laglo— ṭunṭuna ṭun ṭun ṭun! Dhei dhei!
